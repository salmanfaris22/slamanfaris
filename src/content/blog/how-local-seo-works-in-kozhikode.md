Local SEO is how your business shows up when someone nearby searches for what you offer. In Kozhikode that often means appearing on Google Maps, and it depends mostly on three things Google itself names: relevance, distance and prominence.

[Google's own help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) explains these directly. Here is what they mean for a business in Calicut.

## Relevance: does your listing match the search?

A complete profile helps Google understand what you do. Pick the most accurate primary category, list your real services and write a plain description. If you are a dental clinic in Mavoor Road, say so clearly on your profile and your website.

## Distance: you cannot fake this

Google considers how far you are from the person searching, or from the place they named. That is why honest location details matter so much, and why a virtual office is not a shortcut. Google's [guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) require a real location with in-person customer contact, and breaking them can get a listing suspended.

## Prominence: how well known you are

This is the part you can grow. Reviews, mentions on other websites and your general search presence all count. Ask real customers for honest reviews and reply to them. Do not buy reviews or offer rewards for them. Both are against Google's rules.

## Use both names

People search for both Calicut and Kozhikode. Use both naturally on your website, and keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere.

## Add content only you could write

Pages that just swap in a place name do not help anyone. Better content answers real local questions: how delivery works in your area, which locations you visit, what customers usually ask.

## What to do first

1. Check whether you qualify for a Google Business Profile.
2. Claim it, verify it and fill in every field.
3. Match your details on your website and directories.
4. Add real photos of your place and your work.
5. Ask recent happy customers for an honest review.
