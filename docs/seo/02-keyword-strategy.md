# Keyword strategy

**Volumes and difficulty are "Not verified".** The connected Ahrefs account returned "Insufficient plan", so no numbers were invented. Add them later from Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console.

## Rule

One main keyword for each page. `src/lib/services.ts` stops the build if two pages share a keyword, title, description, H1 or FAQ.

## "Best" searches

"Best X in Calicut" is matched by an honest FAQ on the right page ("There is no official ranking..."). "Best" is not used in titles or claims, because your brief bans fake best-in-Kerala claims.
