No backlinks can be bought or automated safely. Paid links and mass directory submissions break Google's spam policies and your own brief. These are real links you control or can earn.

## Tier 4: earned links

| Idea | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Guest post on Kerala tech communities | Real local authority |
| Talks at colleges, GTech, Startup Mission, Cyberpark events | Event pages link to speakers |
| NexGo listed in awesome-go style lists | Earned by a real open-source project |
| Client case studies with their permission | They may link back |
| Malayalam tutorials shared in communities | Original content with few competitors |

## Never do

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy links or Fiverr packages | Spam policy risk |
| Mass directory submissions | Banned by your brief, low value |
| Scripted reviews with keywords | Against Google's review policy |
| Pasting "Best digital marketer" everywhere | Unprovable claim |
